Behind the buzz.

Health Risks

Energy drink consumption has so many risks to keep in mind when consuming it. Drinking energy drinks can create tachycardia, which is the fastening in heart beat. This can destroy your heart.It can also dangerously increase your blood pressure rate. All of this is caused by the amount of caffeine and sugar in these drinks, with these ingredients it is crucial to learn how to consume it with moderation.

Alternatives

The very best alternative to energy drinks is water. It is the best fluid to help keep your body hydrated and energized for homework or even as athletes. Another alternative often recommended are electrolyte drinks. However, with our interview with Dr.Dhubuwala, a cardiologist, he mentions that these drinks are only best through constant control.
